7. Proposed Modifications (Fold-Only)
Modification 1 — Upward-Bent Wingtips
Change: Fold the outer trailing edge of each wing upward by approximately 5 mm to create small elevons.
Hypothesis: This will pitch the nose up slightly, potentially increasing lift if the baseline was diving, but may cause stalling if overdone.
Test plan:

Fold a fresh identical plane.

Conduct five trials with flat wings; record times.

Bend both wingtips up by 5 mm and conduct five trials; record times.

Compare mean times and note any change in flight path.

Modification 2 — Extra Nose Fold (CG Shift)
Change: Fold the very tip of the nose over once more (about 1 cm), making the nose slightly heavier without adding any foreign object.
Hypothesis: Moving the CG forward will reduce stalling and produce a straighter glide, potentially increasing flight time if the baseline was tail-heavy.
Test plan:

Fold a fresh identical plane.

Conduct five trials without the extra nose fold; record times.

Add the extra nose fold and conduct five trials; record times.

Compare mean times and stability.

Validity note: Each modification test uses a freshly folded identical plane so that fold fatigue does not bias the results.
